Privacy Policy

Effective November 8, 2026

Tabletop is a free browser service for playing Bulgarian card games and Backgammon with friends. It is operated by the owner of milenid.com. Guest play does not require a Google account. Google sign-in is optional and is used only for players who want an approved identity and persistent ratings.

Information we collect

If you choose Google sign-in, Tabletop receives and stores your stable Google account identifier, email address, display name, and optional profile-picture URL. Google handles your password; Tabletop never receives or stores it. Google access tokens are used only to obtain your profile during sign-in and are not retained.

Tabletop also stores account approval status, account creation and last-login times, ratings, and rated-match results. Authentication sessions use a random browser cookie; only a cryptographic hash of that session token is stored on the server.

Guest names and session identifiers are stored in the browser and held by the server as needed to operate current rooms and games. The hosting and security infrastructure necessarily receives technical information such as IP address, browser information, request time, and requested URL, and may retain limited diagnostic or security logs.

Tabletop stores limited usage analytics for both guests and signed-in players: display name, a random player identifier, browser-reported country/locale and time zone, visit times and duration, rooms created or joined, games watched or played, game type and variant, completion status, and results. Country is inferred from the browser locale; Tabletop does not send visitor IP addresses to an external geolocation service. Cards, private hands, messages, and authentication credentials are not included in usage reports.

If you use the feedback form, Tabletop stores the category, message, display name, signed-in status, current lobby or table context, environment, application version, and submission time in private files on the game server. Your IP address is used temporarily in server memory to enforce submission limits, but is not written to the feedback files. Do not submit passwords or sensitive personal information.

How we use information

We use the information only to authenticate players, reconnect their sessions, approve rated participants, calculate and display ratings and match history, operate games, understand adoption and game completion, improve the service, diagnose problems, prevent abuse, and protect the service.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or share it with data brokers. Google processes sign-in under Google's own privacy terms. Information may otherwise be disclosed only when necessary to operate hosting infrastructure, comply with law, or protect users and the service.

Public information

Display names, ratings, number of rated games, and rated-match results may be visible to other users. Email addresses, Google identifiers, session tokens, and authentication details are not included in public game or scoreboard data.

Retention and deletion

Sign-in sessions normally expire after 30 days. Usage analytics may be retained for up to 24 months. Feedback files are retained until the operator reviews and deletes them, subject to a 10 MiB storage cap. Approved-account details, ratings, and rated-match history are retained so the scoreboard remains persistent. You may disconnect Google from within Tabletop at any time. To request deletion of your Tabletop account and associated personal data, contact the support address displayed on Tabletop's Google consent screen. Revoking access in your Google Account stops future Google authorization but does not by itself erase existing Tabletop ratings or match records.

Security

Tabletop uses HTTPS, HttpOnly cookies, OAuth authorization-code flow with PKCE, hashed session tokens, restricted administrative access, and data-minimization measures. No Internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Optional AI game opponents

When the operator enables an AI opponent, its public Backgammon board, checker colors, dice and legal moves may be sent to OpenAI or a private Ollama server to choose a move. For Rummy, the bot's own hand, public melds and discards, stock count and opponents' hand counts and opening status may also be sent. Opponents' private cards, stock order, player names, account details and authentication information are excluded. OpenAI API processing is subject to OpenAI's applicable data policies. The game remains available with locally calculated bot moves when AI is disabled or unavailable.

Google user data

Tabletop's use and transfer of information received from Google adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The application requests only basic identity information: OpenID identity, email, and profile.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised. Privacy questions and deletion requests may be sent to the user-support address displayed on the Google authorization screen.
